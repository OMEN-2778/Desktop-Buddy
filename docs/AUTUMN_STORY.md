# 秋 · 枫叶城 — story draft

Status: **draft, waiting on owner.** Nothing is drawn or wired yet. Art first, then gameplay.
Owner-given parts are marked 【定】; everything else is Claude's proposal and may change.

## Frame
- 【定】Small vertical industrial city 枫叶城 (in 枫叶谷). Story-driven; free exploration with
  small discoveries; theme: environmental protection, thought-provoking, not preachy.
- 【定】All autumn a detective stands at the front door with a "!"; missed → next autumn.
  He is investigating a case about a mine owner. Talking to him → his private plane → 枫叶城.
- 【定】Buddy may use the detective's house in the city for every need. She never leaves the
  city on her own (rule for all season maps, see `AGENTS.md`).
- 【定】Friend chats / invites muted during the story. Ending: sunny city, falling maple
  leaves, Buddy gets an office job.
- Length target: prologue + 4 chapters + epilogue, 20–30 min.

## Map (vertical, bottom → top)
1. **矿道与河** — old mine tunnels, the river and a rusty pier; the hidden drain pipe.
2. **工人街** — dorms, canteen (饱腹), 洗衣房 + public bath (清洁), public toilet.
3. **中城广场** — market, notice board, **侦探的家** (bed, bath, kitchen, toilet: all needs),
   benches (精力 small), NPCs to chat (社交).
4. **上城** — 赤岩矿业大楼 (Chen's sealed office on the top floor), town hall.
5. **山顶** — maple nursery, old lookout, the plane's landing strip.
Linked by stairs, a freight lift and a small cable car. Before: grey haze, smoke, brown dead
leaves, people in masks. After: blue sky, red maples, falling leaves.

## Story

### 序章 · 门口的侦探
Autumn. A man in a trench coat stands at our door, "!" over his head.
- 林砚 is looking for 程铁山, who went missing a week ago. His car was found on our street,
  and in our mailbox there is a maple-leaf bookmark with a note in Chen's hand:
  **"等枫叶重新红了，我就回来。"**
- The leaf on the bookmark is brown, not red. 林砚 frowns: "枫叶谷的叶子……以前不是这样的。"
- He invites Buddy: "你是最后一个收到他东西的人。跟我去一趟枫叶城吧。"
- The plane lands on the hilltop strip. Below: a grey city in a valley. 林砚 lends Buddy his old
  family house in the middle town. Free exploration; the main story starts when Buddy first
  walks into the workers' street (or after a short while of exploring).

### 第一章 · 灰色的城
Buddy asks around about Chen. Everyone sees him differently:
- Workers: "程老板是好人，食堂是他盖的，工资从没拖过。"
- 阿禾: "我的白衬衫洗三遍还是灰的。河水都成什么颜色了。"
- 小满 shows his brown leaves: "今年一片红的都没有。"
- The secretary in the company tower: before vanishing, Chen sent river water to be tested.
  The report never came back to the office; it went to the laundry by mistake.
- Buddy finds the report at 阿禾's: the river water is full of heavy metals. Chen knew.
- **城市变化 ①:** 阿禾 gets clean water from the hill spring; white sheets hang in the street
  again. The workers' street gets a little lighter.

### 第二章 · 河底的管子
Where does the dirty water come from? The company says the drain is "filtered".
- Following the red water down to the pier, Buddy finds a pipe that isn't on any map.
- 老鲁 knows about it. He opened that valve every night for twenty years, on orders.
  "我闭上它，明天全班人就没活干。你让我怎么办？"
- Buddy doesn't argue. She brings him 小满's brown leaves. 老鲁 is quiet for a long time, then
  closes the valve himself.
- **城市变化 ②:** the river clears, ducks come back, the pier gets its colour.

### 第三章 · 矿道里的笔记本
- In 林砚's old house Buddy finds a childhood photo of him under a red maple: that tree is in
  the square, dead now. 林砚 left because his mother's cough got worse. He never came back.
- A clue from the bookmark leads into the old mine: Chen's hidden rest room. His notebook:
  - Thirty years ago he dug the first shaft with his own hands and the whole valley thanked him.
  - Ten years ago he saw the leaves turning brown and told himself "再等等".
  - This year he decided to close the smelter. 赵启明 and the town hall said no: the mine pays
    for the whole city. So he disappeared with the evidence, to make outsiders come and look.
- The last page: a sketch of the hilltop nursery.
- Up on the hill, the quiet 园丁老伯 who kept saying "叶子会红的" turns around. It's Chen.
- **城市变化 ③:** Chen stops the smelter's worst furnace; the smoke thins, the sky over the
  upper town turns from grey to pale blue.

### 第四章 · 听证会
The whole city meets in the square. This is the thought-provoking part: nobody is a villain.
- 赵启明: "你们拍拍手走了，三百个工人吃什么？" — he is right too.
- Buddy speaks with the voices she collected while exploring (each discovery and conversation
  adds a line: 阿禾's sheets, 小满's leaves, 老鲁's valve, 林砚's tree). More found → a fuller
  speech, but the outcome is never a fail state.
- The way out is a change, not a shutdown: the mine closes step by step; the smelter becomes a
  metal recycling works (same workers, new job); Chen puts his shares into a fund for clean-up
  and the coughing kids. 赵启明 agrees to run the new works.
- What stays bitter (on purpose): some families still leave; 老鲁 retires and says
  "我这辈子干的活，有一半是在还债。" Then plants a maple.
- **城市变化 ④:** the company tower's chimney goes cold.

### 尾声 · 枫叶红了
- A few days later (screen fades): sun, blue sky, red maples, leaves drifting through every
  layer of the city. 小满 brings Buddy the first red leaf.
- Chen comes down from the hill, "等枫叶重新红了，我就回来" fulfilled.
- **Office job:** the top floor of the old company tower — Chen's sealed office — becomes the
  new 枫叶城环境办公室. Chen offers Buddy the desk by the window: watch the river and the air,
  write the monthly report. Buddy now has a job in 枫叶城 (how it works in the house game:
  open question).
- Last twist: 林砚 compares handwriting. The anonymous letter that hired him to "find 程铁山"
  was written by Chen himself. Chen: "这座城里的人看不见了。得有人从外面来。"
- 林砚 flies home and says he'll come by every autumn, now as a friend (that's why he is at the
  door next autumn too).
