# 秋 · 枫叶城 — story (方案 B, owner-approved)

Status: **story approved by owner (2026-11-01).** Approved story and five environment states now run in the test game;
see `AUTUMN_GAME.md`. Art review remains `/autumn/`; formal game integration is off. Codex: read this whole file

## Owner rules for this story
- 【定】The old miner is **老郑** (renamed from 老周 on 2026-11-03: 茵菲谷 already has a 老周 and ships first).
- 【定】Names: detective **阿简**, mine owner **程铁山**, his daughter **程青**, 老郑, 苏医生.
- 【定】Opening must be natural: no coincidences, no puppet-master twist (v1/v2 drafts were
  rejected as greasy/contrived; story = 方案 B below).
- 【定】**No absolute good or bad.** Nobody is a villain and nobody is simply right. Every NPC
  has their own stance, with a real reason behind it and a real cost to it.
- 【定】**Few NPCs, each one deep.** Five story NPCs only (阿简 程铁山 程青 老郑 苏医生); no
  extra named characters. Passers-by
  are unnamed and never carry the plot.
- Buddy is a listener, not a judge: she carries words between people so they hear each other.
  The city decides; the player never picks "the right side".

## Frame
- 【定】Small vertical industrial city 枫叶城 (in 枫叶谷). Story-driven; free exploration with
  small discoveries; theme: environmental protection, thought-provoking, not preachy.
- 【定】All autumn the detective stands at the front door with a "!"; missed → next autumn.
  He is investigating a case about a mine owner. Talking to him → his private plane → 枫叶城.
- 【定】Buddy may use the detective's house in the city for every need. She never leaves the
  city on her own (rule for all season maps, see `AGENTS.md`).
- 【定】Friend chats / invites muted during the story. Ending: sunny city, falling maple
  leaves, Buddy gets an office job.
- Length target: prologue + 4 chapters + epilogue, 20–30 min.


## Map (vertical, bottom → top)
1. **矿道与河** — old mine tunnels, the river, a rusty pier, the unlisted drain pipe.
2. **工人街** — dorms, canteen (饱腹), laundry + public bath (清洁), public toilet, 苏医生's clinic.
3. **中城广场** — market, notice board, **侦探的家** (bed, bath, kitchen, toilet: all needs),
   benches, NPCs to chat (社交).
4. **上城** — 赤岩矿业大楼 (程铁山's old office on the top floor), town hall.
5. **山顶** — maple nursery, old lookout, the plane's landing strip.
Stairs, a freight lift and a small cable car link the layers. Before: grey haze, smoke, brown
dead leaves. After: blue sky, red maples, leaves falling through every layer.

## Story (方案 B, owner-approved)

### 序章 · 隔壁的空房子
- Summer: a quiet old man (程铁山, unnamed until ch1) rents the house next door, gives Buddy maple syrup, then says "回老家一周". He does not come back.
- Autumn: 阿简 (with "!") is knocking on doors along our street. He was hired by the old man's
  daughter. "我手上只有他二十年前的照片。你认得他，能跟我走一趟吗？"
- Buddy accompanies 阿简. The plane barely lands in the smog;
  阿简 mutters: "降得下来，起不起得来就难说了。" He lends her his old family house.

### 第一章 · 找人
- 程青 at the company: polite, tired. "他说要关矿，说完就关机了。"
- 老郑 at the canteen: "老程跑不远，他舍不得那几口井。"
- 苏医生, her clinic full of coughing kids, gets cagey when Buddy mentions 程.
- Clues point to the old miners' hut on the hill.
- **变化 ①:** after Buddy passes on what she saw at the clinic, 程青 has the ore trucks covered
  and the streets watered. Dust settles on the workers' street. She knows it's not enough.

### 第二章 · 山上的老人
- 程铁山 is at the hut, fine, just stubborn. "你那条街的枫叶，是红的。"
- 阿简 phones 程青: found. Case closed. The plane can't take off in the haze, so he stays.
- Father won't come down until the mine closes; daughter: "你开矿的时候，问过谁吗？"
- 程铁山 and 老郑 close the old tailings drain 程 built himself.
- **变化 ②:** the river clears. Cost: 老郑's night shift is gone.

### 第三章 · 举报信
- An inspection team is coming because of anonymous letters. The city panics; 老郑 wants to find
  "那个告状的". 苏医生 tells only Buddy it was her; the mine pays for her clinic.
- Buddy doesn't spill it. 苏医生 decides to tell 老郑 herself; he walks out without a word.
- 程青 has to choose: tidy up for the inspectors, or open the books. She shuts the old furnace.
- **变化 ③:** smoke thins, the upper town sees blue.

### 第四章 · 账本
- 程青 opens the books at a meeting in the square. Debts, wages, the river reports.
- Plan the city makes together: the mine winds down over years, part becomes a recycling works;
  the clinic gets a public fund. Costs stay: some families leave; 老郑 retires; father and
  daughter still don't fully agree. 老郑 comes back to the clinic with his grandson.
- **变化 ④:** the chimney goes cold.

### 尾声
- Sun, red maples, leaves falling through every layer. Buddy meets everyone in the square.
- 程青 offers Buddy a desk in the new office that publishes the river/air report each month:
  "大家都跟你说过话。你写的，他们会看。"
- 阿简's plane can finally take off. "明年秋天，我再来看看。"
