# 秋 · 枫叶城 — story draft v2

Status: **draft, waiting on owner.** Nothing is drawn or wired yet. Art first, then gameplay.
Owner-given parts are marked 【定】; everything else is Claude's proposal and may change.

## Owner rules for this story
- 【定】The detective is called **阿简**. Owner likes the later chapters; the opening and
  early chapters read as greasy/contrived and will be rewritten (owner is asking another AI).
- 【定】**No absolute good or bad.** Nobody is a villain and nobody is simply right. Every NPC
  has their own stance, with a real reason behind it and a real cost to it.
- 【定】**Few NPCs, each one deep.** Five story NPCs only; no extra named characters. Passers-by
  are unnamed and never carry the plot.
- Buddy is a listener, not a judge: she carries words between people so they hear each other.
  The city decides; the player never picks "the right side".

## Frame
- 【定】Small vertical industrial city 枫叶城 (in 枫叶谷). Story-driven; free exploration with
  small discoveries; theme: environmental protection, thought-provoking, not preachy.
- 【定】All autumn the detective stands at the front door with a "!"; missed → next autumn.
  He is investigating a case about a mine owner. Talking to him → his private plane → 枫叶城.
- 【定】Buddy may use the detective's house in the city for every need. She never leaves the
  city on her own (rule for all season maps, see `AGENTS.md`).
- 【定】Friend chats / invites muted during the story. Ending: sunny city, falling maple
  leaves, Buddy gets an office job.
- Length target: prologue + 4 chapters + epilogue, 20–30 min.

## Cast (5)
| 角色 | 立场 | 他的道理 | 他的代价/盲点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **阿简** 侦探 | 真相必须公开 | 他母亲当年咳得厉害，全家才搬走；他觉得沉默就是帮凶 | 走了二十年，回来就下判断；城里人问他："你又不在这儿过日子。" |
| **程铁山** 矿场老板（失踪） | 关掉冶炼厂 | 城是他挖出来的，叶子变褐他看了十年，再不关就晚了 | 污染是他赚的钱；他用失踪、匿名信把外人卷进来，替自己的良心找证人；想关就关，没问过工人 |
| **赵启明** 副总 | 矿场不能停 | 三百个工人、他们的房贷孩子，公司还欠着银行的钱；他天天在算这笔账 | 他也早知道河水有问题，选择不看；但最后真正想出转型办法、扛下债的人是他 |
| **老鲁** 夜班工头 | 护着自己的班组 | 二十年每晚开排污阀，是命令，也是全班人的饭碗 | 他最清楚河变成了什么样；关阀门等于砸兄弟们的饭碗 |
| **阿禾** 洗衣房老板娘 | 要干净的河 | 衬衫洗不白，儿子一直咳嗽 | 她的生意全靠矿工；矿停了，第一个关门的就是她的店 |

The quiet gardener on the hill is 程铁山 in hiding (not a sixth character).

## Map (vertical, bottom → top)
1. **矿道与河** — old mine tunnels, the river, a rusty pier, the unlisted drain pipe.
2. **工人街** — dorms, canteen (饱腹), 阿禾's laundry + public bath (清洁), public toilet.
3. **中城广场** — market, notice board, **侦探的家** (bed, bath, kitchen, toilet: all needs),
   benches, NPCs to chat (社交).
4. **上城** — 赤岩矿业大楼 (Chen's sealed office on the top floor), town hall.
5. **山顶** — maple nursery, old lookout, the plane's landing strip.
Stairs, a freight lift and a small cable car link the layers. Before: grey haze, smoke, brown
dead leaves. After: blue sky, red maples, leaves falling through every layer.

## Story

### 序章 · 门口的侦探
- 阿简 is looking for 程铁山, missing for a week. Chen's car was found on our street; in our
  mailbox there is a maple bookmark in his hand: **"等枫叶重新红了，我就回来。"** The leaf is
  brown.
- 阿简: "你是最后一个收到他东西的人。跟我去一趟枫叶城吧。" The plane lands on the hilltop.
  阿简 lends Buddy his old family house. Free exploration; the story starts when Buddy first
  walks into the workers' street.

### 第一章 · 每个人眼里的程老板
Buddy asks around. Nobody describes the same man:
- 赵启明: "他突然说要关厂，说完人就没了。三百个人的饭碗，他一句话就想砸？"
- 老鲁: "他二十年前跟我们一起下井。他要是想关厂，总有他的道理……可我们怎么办。"
- 阿禾: "他欠这条河的。"（停一下）"……不过我这店，也是靠他的工人撑着。"
- 阿简 finds a water test Chen ordered before vanishing; the report went to 阿禾's laundry by
  mistake. Heavy metals in the river. 阿简 wants to post it on the notice board right away.
  阿禾 stops him: "贴出去，明天就没人敢来洗衣服了，河也不会自己变干净。"
- Buddy's part: carry 阿禾's words to 阿简 and his to her. They agree to wait until they know
  where the water comes from.
- **城市变化 ①:** 阿禾 starts fetching spring water from the hill; white sheets hang in the
  street again. Small, and paid for by her own extra work.

### 第二章 · 河底的管子
- Down at the pier Buddy finds a pipe that is on no map. 老鲁 knows it. "我闭上它，明天全班
  就没活干。你让我怎么办？"
- 阿简 pushes: this is evidence. 赵启明 arrives: closing the drain means the smelter must slow
  down, and slower means wages drop. Both are right.
- Buddy does not argue. She brings 老鲁 what she heard: 阿禾's son coughs; the crew's own kids
  play by this river. 老鲁 closes the valve himself, then walks off to tell his crew their
  night shifts are cut. He doesn't talk to Buddy for a while.
- **城市变化 ②:** the river clears, ducks return. The night shift ends; the canteen is quieter.

### 第三章 · 山顶的园丁
- In 阿简's house Buddy finds his childhood photo under a red maple in the square, dead now.
  阿简 admits he never told anyone here why his family left; he just left.
- Chen's hidden room in the old mine: his notebook. Thirty years ago he dug the first shaft and
  the valley thanked him; ten years ago he saw the leaves brown and wrote "再等等"; this year he
  decided to close the smelter, was refused, and vanished with the evidence.
- The last page sketches the hilltop nursery. The quiet gardener who kept saying "叶子会红的"
  is Chen.
- He is not sorry in the way people expect: "我知道你们都恨我。挖矿的时候恨，关矿的时候也
  恨。" 阿简 is angry he was used; Chen: "我不找外人来，这城里谁肯先开口？" Both have a point.
- **城市变化 ③:** Chen and 赵启明 shut the worst furnace together, not as friends. The smoke
  thins and the upper town's sky turns pale blue.

### 第四章 · 广场上的会
The whole city meets in the square. Nobody wins the argument.
- 赵启明: "你们拍拍手走了，三百个工人吃什么？"
- 阿禾: "我的店也会倒。可我儿子得喘气。"
- 老鲁: "活是我们干的，债也该我们一起还。"
- 阿简 is told "你又不在这儿过日子" and, for once, does not answer.
- Buddy relays what each person told her in private (discoveries and talks add lines; the more
  she found, the more people hear each other). It is never a fail state.
- What the city chooses together: the mine closes step by step; 赵启明's plan turns the
  smelter into a metal recycling works (same crews, less pay at first); Chen puts his shares
  into a clean-up fund. Costs stay on screen: some families still move away; 阿禾's laundry
  has fewer customers; 老鲁 retires: "我这辈子干的活，有一半是在还债。" Then he plants a maple.
- **城市变化 ④:** the tower's chimney goes cold.

### 尾声 · 枫叶红了
- Days later: sun, red maples, leaves drifting through every layer. Chen comes down from the
  hill: "等枫叶重新红了" is kept.
- **Office job:** Chen's sealed office on top of the tower becomes the new environment office,
  run jointly by Chen and 赵启明. They offer Buddy the window desk: watch the river and the air,
  write the monthly report that everyone in the city can read.
- 阿简 compares handwriting: the anonymous letter that hired him was written by Chen. He is
  angry for a moment, then laughs. Chen: "这城里的人看不见了，得有人从外面来。"
- 阿简 flies home and says he'll come by every autumn, now as a friend.

# 方案 B（Claude 重写，与上面的 v2 二选一或混用）
Goal: a natural opening with no coincidences, no puppet-master twist; mystery solved early,
the real story is the city's choice. 5 NPCs.

## Cast
| 角色 | 立场 | 理由 | 代价/盲点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **阿简** 侦探 | 只管查案，不想掺和 | 枫叶城人，多年前离开；这是份工作 | 案子破了却走不了（雾霾，飞机起不来），被迫看清老家 |
| **何振东** 矿场老板，退休 | 关矿 | 夏天住在我家隔壁，看到这里的枫叶是红的，自己家乡的不是 | 当年开矿没问过谁，现在关矿也不问；把烂摊子丢给女儿 |
| **何青** 他女儿，现任总经理 | 慢慢改，不能停 | 三百户人家、银行贷款都压在她身上 | 一直在拖，"等下个季度" |
| **老周** 老矿工，班组长 | 保住饭碗 | 一辈子只会这个，徒弟们要养家 | 知道河的事，装作不知道 |
| **苏医生** 小诊所医生 | 必须让外面知道 | 诊所里咳嗽的孩子越来越多 | 诊所的钱是矿上出的；匿名举报信是她写的，不敢认 |

## 序章 · 隔壁的空房子
- Summer: a quiet old man rents the house next door, gives Buddy maple syrup, and before leaving
  "回老家一周" asks her to water his maple sapling and leaves the key. He doesn't come back.
- Autumn: 阿简 (with "!") is knocking on doors along our street. He was hired by the old man's
  daughter. "我手上只有他二十年前的照片。你认得他，能跟我走一趟吗？"
- Buddy brings the sapling (she promised to look after it). The plane barely lands in the smog;
  阿简 mutters: "降得下来，起不起得来就难说了。" He lends her his old family house.

## 第一章 · 找人
- 何青 at the company: polite, tired. "他说要关矿，说完就关机了。"
- 老周 at the canteen: "老何跑不远，他舍不得那几口井。"
- 苏医生, clinic full of coughing kids, gets cagey when Buddy mentions 何.
- Clues point to the old miners' hut on the hill.
- **变化 ①:** after Buddy passes on what she saw at the clinic, 何青 has the ore trucks covered
  and the streets watered. Dust settles on the workers' street. She knows it's not enough.

## 第二章 · 山上的老人
- 何振东 is at the hut, fine, just stubborn. "你那条街的枫叶，是红的。"
- 阿简 phones 何青: found. Case closed. The plane can't take off in the haze, so he stays.
- Father won't come down until the mine closes; daughter: "你开矿的时候，问过谁吗？"
- 何振东 and 老周 close the old tailings drain 何 built himself.
- **变化 ②:** the river clears. Cost: 老周's night shift is gone.

## 第三章 · 举报信
- An inspection team is coming because of anonymous letters. The city panics; 老周 wants to find
  "那个告状的". 苏医生 tells only Buddy it was her; the mine pays for her clinic.
- Buddy doesn't spill it. 苏医生 decides to tell 老周 herself; he walks out without a word.
- 何青 has to choose: tidy up for the inspectors, or open the books. She shuts the old furnace.
- **变化 ③:** smoke thins, the upper town sees blue.

## 第四章 · 账本
- 何青 opens the books at a meeting in the square. Debts, wages, the river reports.
- Plan the city makes together: the mine winds down over years, part becomes a recycling works;
  the clinic gets a public fund. Costs stay: some families leave; 老周 retires; father and
  daughter still don't fully agree. 老周 comes back to the clinic with his grandson.
- **变化 ④:** the chimney goes cold.

## 尾声
- Sun, red maples, leaves falling through every layer. Buddy plants the sapling in the square.
- 何青 offers Buddy a desk in the new office that publishes the river/air report each month:
  "大家都跟你说过话。你写的，他们会看。"
- 阿简's plane can finally take off. "明年秋天，我再来看看那棵树。"
